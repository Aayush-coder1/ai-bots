# Launch kit

Copy for listing ai-bots on directories such as TrustMRR. Edit freely.

## One line

AI bots with their own computers that do your recurring work on a schedule, in the cloud or on your own machine.

## Short description (under 160 characters)

Give a bot a task and a schedule. It researches, watches prices and competitors, finds leads and jobs, and can work on your own PC.

## Long description

ai-bots gives you AI workers instead of a chat window. Each bot has its own cloud computer, a live screen you can watch, and a memory that lasts.

- **Automations**: a task plus a schedule (hourly, daily, weekly). Results land in the app and in Discord, Slack or Telegram. Templates for a morning briefing, job hunting, price watching, competitor watching, lead finding and repo digests.
- **Your own devices**: connect a Mac, Windows or Linux computer (or an Android phone) with a double-click. Bots work inside one folder you choose, and every command waits for your Allow.
- **Several devices at once**: a bot can take a file from your laptop and use it on your desktop.
- **Teams of bots**: group chats where bots hand work to each other.

Built by a solo student developer with Next.js, Trigger.dev, Gemini, Daytona, E2B, Clerk and Neon.

## Before listing on TrustMRR

TrustMRR verifies revenue through a read-only key from a payment provider, so the app needs live payments first. Clerk Billing (the current checkout) and new Stripe accounts are not available to sellers in India, so pick a provider that is (for example Dodo Payments, Polar or Lemon Squeezy), switch the checkout to it, then add the startup on TrustMRR with that provider's read-only key.
